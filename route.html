🎟 Buy before you go (everything prepayable, in one place)

  1. Parking (Secaucus Junction Lot 1, 675 New County Rd): prepay ~$6/day (2-day min) at go.lazparking.com or OnAirParking.com — search “Secaucus Junction”, pick Jul 29 → 31. Gate rate without prepay: ~$10–15/day.
  2. Train tickets (Secaucus ↔ Penn Station): download the official NJ Transit app and buy 4 one-ways now — iPhone App Store · Android Play Store · how MyTix works. Activate each ticket just before boarding.
  3. Subway (D train etc.): nothing to buy — every turnstile takes tap-to-pay: your phone wallet or contactless credit card, $2.90/ride charged automatically. Details: omny.info. Just make sure each of you has a card in Apple/Google Pay.
  4. Jazz tickets: use the “Tickets / reserve” button on each show's pin on the main map — Wednesday late sets rarely sell out, but Thursday's Blue Note 10:30 and Birdland sets are worth booking today.
  5. Live train/subway times when you're out: Subway — mta.info/schedules (pick the D line for live arrivals) — though the fastest habit is the green “Open in Google Maps” button on each leg below: it shows the next departing trains in real time. For the Friday train back to your car: NJ Transit DepartureVision — pick “New York Penn Station” and you'll see exactly which track and when for the next Secaucus-bound train.

  1. Take I-80 East across New Jersey.
  2. Follow signs for NJ-3 East / Secaucus (or NJ Turnpike exit 15X).
  3. GPS destination: Secaucus Junction Station Parking, 675 New County Road, Secaucus NJ — the big daily lot at the train station. Parking allowed 24/7 (verified on NJ Transit's official station page).
  4. 💡 Prepay before you leave (~$6/day, 2-day minimum) at go.lazparking.com or OnAirParking for Secaucus Junction Lot 1 — otherwise figure $10–15/day at the gate.
  5. Park, take your backpacks, walk into the station. Done driving until Friday.

Leg 2 — 🚆 NJ Transit train: Secaucus → Penn Station (1 stop, ~10 min)

  1. Buy tickets ahead of time in the “NJ Transit” app (free, App Store): One-Way, Secaucus → New York Penn Station. Buy all four now (two out Wednesday, two back Friday) — activate each one just before boarding.
  2. Inside the station, follow the signs that say “To New York.” Every train on that platform goes to Penn Station — it's the next and last stop, about 10 minutes.
  3. Show/scan the activated app ticket at the fare gates.
  4. You arrive inside Penn Station, 34th St, Manhattan. Take any escalator up to the street.
