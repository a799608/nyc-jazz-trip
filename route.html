🗽 Route Guide — Wed Jul 29 → Fri Jul 31

Liverpool vs Wrexham 7:30 PM · park in NJ · one train in · one subway line all night

Leg 1 — 🚗 Drive: Wilkes-Barre → Secaucus Junction (~1 hr 50 min)

  1. Take I-80 East across New Jersey.
  2. Follow signs for NJ-3 East / Secaucus (or NJ Turnpike exit 15X).
  3. GPS destination: Secaucus Junction Station Parking, 675 New County Road, Secaucus NJ — the big daily lot at the train station. Parking allowed 24/7 (verified on NJ Transit's official station page).
  4. 💡 Prepay before you leave (~$6/day, 2-day minimum) at go.lazparking.com or OnAirParking for Secaucus Junction Lot 1 — otherwise figure $10–15/day at the gate.
  5. Park, take your backpacks, walk into the station. Done driving until Friday.

Leg 2 — 🚆 NJ Transit train: Secaucus → Penn Station (1 stop, ~10 min)

  1. Buy tickets ahead of time in the “NJ Transit” app (free, App Store): One-Way, Secaucus → New York Penn Station. Buy all four now (two out Wednesday, two back Friday) — activate each one just before boarding.
  2. Inside the station, follow the signs that say “To New York.” Every train on that platform goes to Penn Station — it's the next and last stop, about 10 minutes.
  3. Show/scan the activated app ticket at the fare gates.
  4. You arrive inside Penn Station, 34th St, Manhattan. Take any escalator up to the street.

Leg 3 — 🎒 Walk to hotel, drop the bags

  1. Walk to your hotel and leave the backpacks there (even if the room isn't ready — the front desk holds bags).
  2. Yankee Stadium does not allow backpacks. Bring only pocket stuff + phone tickets.
  3. Penn Station is at 34th St & 7th Ave — most Midtown hotels on the map are under 10 minutes' walk.

Leg 4 — ⚾ Subway to the match: Herald Sq → Yankee Stadium (~25 min)

  1. Go to 34 St–Herald Sq station (34th & 6th — one block from the PATH exit; orange B/D/F/M globes).
  2. Tap your credit card at the turnstile (same tap-to-pay, $2.90).
  3. Take the D train, direction “Uptown / Bronx.” Follow signs that say Uptown.
  4. Ride to 161 St–Yankee Stadium (~25 min). The stadium is directly outside.
  5. Kickoff 7:30 PM — be on the train by 6:30 to walk in comfortably.

Leg 5 — 🎷 After the final whistle: one train does everything

  1. Back into 161 St station, take the D train, direction “Downtown / Brooklyn.”
  2. For Midtown jazz (Jazzcultural, Birdland area): off at 7 Av or 47–50 Sts Rockefeller Ctr (~20 min).
  3. For Village jazz (The Django is near, Vanguard/Blue Note area): stay on to W 4 St–Washington Sq (~30 min).
  4. Back to the hotel later: any D/F/M train to 34 St–Herald Sq. Subway runs all night.
⏰ The one curfew that matters (Friday-morning car pickup is easy; this is about IF you'd sleep in NJ — skip if hotel is in Manhattan): NJ Transit is NOT 24-hour. The last weeknight train from Penn Station that stops at Secaucus leaves around 1:20 AM (check "DepartureVision" in the NJ Transit app that night). With a Manhattan hotel this doesn't affect you at all — the car just sleeps in Secaucus until Friday.
