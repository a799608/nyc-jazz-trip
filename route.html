Leg 2 — 🚆 PATH train: Harrison → 33rd St, Manhattan (~25 min)

  3. Relax ~25 minutes. Stay on until the last stop. You can't miss it — the train ends there.
  4. Come up the stairs: you're at 33rd St & 6th Ave, Midtown Manhattan.
